## Computational Design

- **Seek-CAD: Training-Free Parametric CAD Generation via DeepSeek-R1 (ICLR 2026)**: Researchers from Fudan University and Donghua University introduced Seek-CAD, a training-free framework for generating 3D parametric CAD models by running DeepSeek-R1 locally with a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) strategy over a corpus of CAD Python code. Its self-refinement loop incorporates both visual and Chain-of-Thought feedback to iteratively correct generated geometry, achieving 92% accuracy on complex models — a 45% reduction in geometric error over prior baselines. Its training-free design means any lab with a local GPU can adopt it without expensive fine-tuning.
  - Sources: [Seek-CAD – arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17702) · [ICLR 2026 poster](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10009633) · [AI Models FYI summary](https://www.aimodels.fyi/papers/arxiv/seek-cad-self-refined-generative-modeling-3d)

- **Mamba-CAD: State Space Models for Industrial CAD Generation (AAAI 2026)**: Mamba-CAD applies structured state space models (SSMs) to the problem of generating long parametric CAD sequences — the kind needed for complex, fine-grained industrial components. The encoder-decoder architecture uses Mamba for latent representation pre-training on 77,078 industrial CAD models, then guides a GAN to produce realistic CAD sequences. This addresses a known limitation of transformer-based CAD generators, which struggle with long command sequences.
  - Sources: [Mamba-CAD – arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00439) · [AAAI 2026 paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32531) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390716842_Mamba-CAD_State_Space_Model_for_3D_Computer-Aided_Design_Generative_Modeling)

- **GenCAD: Image-Conditioned Parametric CAD from a Single Photo**: GenCAD uses a pipeline of autoregressive transformer encoders, contrastive learning, and latent diffusion to reconstruct editable parametric CAD programs from a single image. Industries from aerospace to medical devices are piloting the approach to dramatically reduce manual re-modeling time when physical parts need to be reverse-engineered into editable CAD files.
  - Sources: [GenCAD overview – Sesame Disk](https://sesamedisk.com/gencad-image-conditioned-cad-generation/) · [AI-Generated Parametric Models 2026 – MeshMayhem](https://www.meshmayhem.com/post/ai-generated-parametric-models-the-future-of-cad-and-3d-printing-2026-guide)

- **Text-to-CAD Tools Reaching Practitioner Maturity**: Commercial tools including AdamCAD (natural language → parametric 3D model with adjustable dimension sliders, STL/CAD export) and Backflip AI (scan-to-parametric conversion of STL meshes into editable models) are reaching production-level usability in 2026. These lower the skill barrier for rapid prototyping and are being used in design-for-manufacturing pipelines where iteration speed matters more than expert CAD proficiency.
  - Sources: [Best AI CAD Software 2026 – The CAD Hub](https://thecadhub.com/blog/smarter-cad-with-ai/) · [AI-Generated Parametric Models 2026 – MeshMayhem](https://www.meshmayhem.com/post/ai-generated-parametric-models-the-future-of-cad-and-3d-printing-2026-guide)

Real-time or near real-time structural feedback is enabling designers to explore geometry, performance, and manufacturability together for the first time.

## Computational Sciences

- **AlphaEvolve Improves Strassen's Algorithm After 56 Years**: The most striking computational result of May 2026 is AlphaEvolve's discovery of a matrix-multiplication algorithm that uses 48 scalar operations to multiply two 4×4 complex-valued matrices — beating the Strassen-derived record that had stood since 1969. The approach uses Gemini to generate algorithmic variants, automated evaluators to verify correctness, and an evolutionary loop to select and mutate the best candidates across thousands of parallel evaluations.
  - Sources: [AlphaEvolve – Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) · [AlphaEvolve arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131) · [GitHub results repo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphaevolve_results)

- **SOPTX: Multi-Backend Topology Optimization Framework (Communications in Computational Physics)**: SOPTX is an open-source Python framework for topology optimization that decouples the physical analysis from the optimization logic via a clean modular architecture (material → solver → filter → optimizer). It supports NumPy, PyTorch, and JAX backends, enabling automatic differentiation for sensitivity computation and GPU-accelerated compliance minimization across arbitrary mesh dimensions. Publication in *Communications in Computational Physics* (vol. 40, no. 2, pp. 525–572, 2026) establishes it as a community reference implementation.
  - Sources: [SOPTX – Global-Sci.com](https://www.global-sci.com/cicp/article/view/24166) · [SOPTX – arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02438) · [ArxivLens summary](https://arxivlens.com/paperview/details/soptx-a-high-performance-multi-backend-framework-for-topology-optimization-8121-f52084b1)

- **Dual Physics-Informed Neural Network for Topology Optimization (DPNN-TO)**: A ScienceDirect paper introduced DPNN-TO, which merges PINNs with traditional SIMP-based topology optimization by training two complementary networks — one enforcing equilibrium PDEs and one managing material density updates. Crucially, DPNN-TO eliminates the need for large labeled training datasets and analytical sensitivity derivations, making it accessible for problems where traditional FEM analysis is too costly or intractable.
  - Sources: [Dual PINN for TO – ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021999126000161) · [Topology Optimization via ML review – Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/jcde/article/10/4/1736/7223974)

- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks with Re-Initialization for Stiff Fluids**: A *Scientific Reports* paper (Nature Publishing Group) addressed a key failure mode of PINNs on stiff fluid problems: convergence to physically implausible local minima in high Reynolds-number or phase-transition scenarios. The proposed "re-initialization" training strategy periodically resets network parameters, allowing the model to escape local minima and recover physically consistent solutions — with no change to the network architecture itself.
  - Sources: [PINNs with Re-Initialization – Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-99354-5) · [Review of Deep Learning for Fluid Simulation – Royal Society](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspa/article/479/2275/20230058/54559/Current-and-emerging-deep-learning-methods-for-the)

- **AlphaEvolve Optimizes Quantum Circuits on Willow Processor**: Applied to quantum computing, AlphaEvolve generated circuits for molecular simulations on Google's Willow quantum processor with error rates 10× lower than conventional hand-optimized circuits. This enables first-of-a-kind experimental demonstrations in first-principles quantum chemistry simulations, bridging the gap between near-term noisy quantum hardware and scientifically meaningful calculations.
  - Sources: [AlphaEvolve – Google DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) · [Google I/O 2026 Research](https://research.google/blog/a-new-era-of-innovation-google-research-at-io-2026/)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **Bistable Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuator with Multiple Deformation Modes (BOSPA)**: Published in *Soft Robotics* (SAGE, 2026), this actuator is built from a 3D-printed inflatable Miura-origami tube with two parallel air chambers and elastic ring reinforcements that induce bistability. Depending on which chamber is pressurized and in what sequence, the actuator can independently produce linear extension, pure bending, or coupled bending/extension — giving a single pneumatic unit access to multiple locomotion gaits. This multi-modal reconfigurability from a single simple actuator design is notable for reducing system complexity in soft-legged robots.
  - Sources: [BOSPA – SAGE Journals](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024)

- **Harvard Rotational Multimaterial Printing for Asymmetric Pneumatic Soft Robots**: The Harvard *Advanced Materials* paper described above is equally significant for soft robotics: by printing asymmetrical core-shell filaments with a continuously rotating nozzle, stiffness gradients and actuation asymmetries are encoded directly into the material microstructure. The Fermat-spiral path planning integrates seamlessly with custom geometry, allowing programmable twist-bend coupling without manual assembly or external constraints.
  - Sources: [Rotational Multimaterial 3D Printing – Wiley Advanced Materials](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.202510141) · [Harvard 3D Printing – Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/harvard-researchers-develop-novel-3d-printing-method-for-soft-robotics-rotational-multi-material-method-creates-muscle-like-structures-that-can-be-programmed-to-twist-lift-or-bend)

- **Unified Spring-Based Framework for Soft Inflatable Fabric Actuators (Scientific Reports)**: A team from Hellenic Mediterranean University published a unified taxonomy and spring-based modeling framework for soft inflatable fabric actuators. They model a stiffening actuator as the fundamental "unit cell" and show that elongating, contracting, and bending actuators can be derived from series/parallel combinations of this unit — validated with high-fidelity FEM simulations. The framework dramatically simplifies task-specific actuator design for wearables, biomedical devices, and adaptive structures by replacing case-by-case FEM studies with modular composition.
  - Sources: [Unified Framework – Scientific Reports / Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-25643-8) · [PMC full text](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12644749/)

- **Princeton Soft-Rigid Hybrid Robots: Liquid Crystal Elastomers + Origami + Flexible Electronics**: Princeton researchers combined 3D-printed liquid crystal elastomers (LCEs) with flexible electronics and origami-like folding to create soft-rigid hybrid robots that locomote without external pneumatic controls or motors — actuated purely by LCE thermal or photothermal response. The origami folds create geometric amplification, converting small material strains into large shape changes, while the embedded flexible circuits enable onboard sensing.
  - Sources: [Multimaterial 3D Printing Soft Robots – RenderHub](https://www.renderhub.com/blog/soft-robotics-flexible-3d-printed-machines-and-the-next-revolution) · [Multi-Material AM of Soft Robots Review 2026 – Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adrr.202500064)

- **Impulsive Actuation for Fast Soft Robots (npj Robotics)**: A *npj Robotics* paper introduced impulsive actuation strategies for soft robots — short, high-energy pressure pulses rather than sustained inflation — enabling jumping, impact-driven locomotion, and rapid reconfiguration speeds that steady-state pneumatic systems cannot achieve. This opens up soft robotics for dynamic tasks such as obstacle vaulting and fast assembly that previously required rigid actuators.
  - Sources: [Impulsive Actuation – npj Robotics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44182-025-00045-0)

- **Origami-Inspired Electrohydraulic Soft Actuators for Multi-Modal Motion (AIP Journal)**: A paper in *Journal of Applied Physics* demonstrated origami-inspired electrohydraulic soft actuators capable of jumping, crawling at 1.23 body lengths per second, and grasping — all from a single flexible device architecture. The electrostatic-hydraulic driving mechanism avoids bulky pneumatic pumps entirely, making the system fully self-contained and suitable for untethered micro-scale deployment.
  - Sources: [Origami Electrohydraulic Actuators – AIP Publishing](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jap/article/139/2/025109/3377304/Origami-inspired-electrohydraulic-soft-actuators)

- **Multi-Material AM of Soft Robotic Systems: Comprehensive Review (Advanced Robotics Research 2026)**: A Wiley *Advanced Robotics Research* review synthesizes the state of multi-material additive manufacturing for soft robotics, covering embedded actuation, distributed sensing, and hybrid stiff-soft structures. It identifies eight-nozzle multi-material deposition as a near-term capability enabler and highlights continuous fiber composites and LCEs as the two most impactful material classes for the next generation of printable soft robots.
  - Sources: [Multi-Material AM Soft Robots Review – Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adrr.202500064)

## Biology & Medical Research
