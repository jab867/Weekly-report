## Computational Design

- **Mamba-CAD: State Space Models for Parametric CAD Generation (AAAI 2026)**: Mamba-CAD applies the Mamba state space model (SSM) architecture—previously celebrated for long-sequence efficiency in NLP—to the problem of generative 3D CAD modeling. The encoder-decoder framework uses Mamba for pre-training a latent representation of CAD construction sequences, then conditions a GAN to generate novel sequences decoded back into parametric CAD. A new dataset of 77,078 industry-grade CAD models with long parametric sequences was released alongside the model, specifically targeting the regime where transformer memory costs become prohibitive.
  - Sources: [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00439) · [AAAI Proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32531) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390716842_Mamba-CAD_State_Space_Model_for_3D_Computer-Aided_Design_Generative_Modeling)

- **ReCAD: Reinforcement Learning Meets Parametric CAD with Vision-Language Models**: ReCAD uses RL-enhanced fine-tuning of vision-language models to generate and refine parametric CAD models from images or sketches, closing the loop between visual intent and editable engineering geometry. The approach addresses a persistent gap: existing text-to-3D tools produce meshes that are not parametrically editable or dimensionally constrained for manufacturing, whereas ReCAD outputs CAD construction histories that downstream tools can modify.
  - Sources: [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06328) · [AI CAD Trends 2026](https://shalindesigns.com/blog/cad-design-trends-2026-ai-cloud-digital-twin/)

## Computational Sciences

- **SimTO: Simulation-Driven Topology Optimization for Custom Soft Robotic Grippers (arXiv Jan 2026)**: SimTO is a framework that automates topology optimization for soft pneumatic grippers by extracting load cases directly from a contact-physics simulator rather than requiring manual specification. Given an arbitrary feature-rich target object (e.g., a broccoli floret, a gear with sharp teeth, a fragile coral), SimTO produces customized gripper morphologies with fine-grained structural features tailored to the object's geometry. The insight is that for soft grippers, the hundreds of unpredictable contact forces during grasping are unknowable a priori—so SimTO lets the simulator discover them automatically, removing the dominant bottleneck in applying topology optimization to compliant robot design.
  - Sources: [arXiv Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19098) · [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19098)

- **PeTTO: GPU-Accelerated Topology Optimization with Pseudo-Transient Methods (arXiv Sep 2025)**: Researchers from Peking University's School of Mathematical Sciences introduced PeTTO, a topology optimization framework that reformulates the PDE-constrained optimization problem as a set of time-dependent PDEs solvable via pseudo-transient methods, enabling GPU-native acceleration using automatic differentiation. PeTTO achieves 40–50x speedup on a single GPU compared to desktop CPU solvers for the same problems, covering 2D and 3D cases in mechanical and thermal physics for both single-material and multi-material designs—making large-scale topology optimization dramatically more accessible.
  - Sources: [arXiv PeTTO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06971) · [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.06971)

- **Lagrangian Topology-Conscious Physics-Informed Neural Networks (LT-PINN)**: Published in Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering in 2026, LT-PINN introduces a Lagrangian physics-informed neural network framework specifically designed for boundary-focused engineering optimization problems. Unlike standard PINNs that treat domain interiors and boundaries uniformly, LT-PINN concentrates representational capacity at evolving boundaries where design decisions concentrate, improving convergence behavior for topology optimization tasks.
  - Sources: [ASTESJ PINN Approach](https://www.astesj.com/v06/i04/p27/) · [Ansys Topology Background](https://www.ansys.com/simulation-topics/what-is-topology-optimization)

- **Unified Phase-Field Fourier Neural Network for Topology Optimization**: A recent arXiv preprint proposes a unified framework combining phase-field methods with Fourier neural networks for topology optimization, enabling smooth, differentiable representations of material boundaries that interface naturally with both neural network training and physical simulation constraints. The Fourier representation avoids checkerboard instabilities common in density-based SIMP methods while remaining computationally efficient at high resolutions.
  - Sources: [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.14623) · [Neural Networks for Topology Optimization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.17957)

- **4D Topology Optimization: Integrated Structure and Self-Actuation of Soft Bodies**: Research on 4D topology optimization integrates structural design with self-actuation programming for soft bodies executing dynamic motions. This extends classical topology optimization—which finds optimal material distribution for static loading—to simultaneously optimize material layout and spatially varying actuation fields, so that the resulting soft body executes prescribed motions when actuated, a key enabling capability for autonomous soft robots and shape-morphing structures.
  - Sources: [arXiv 4D Topology](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.00905) · [SimTO Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19098)

- **JAX-FEM: Differentiable GPU-Accelerated Finite Element Solver**: JAX-FEM provides a fully differentiable GPU-accelerated 3D finite element solver built on JAX, enabling automatic gradient computation through finite element simulations for inverse design and mechanistic data science. The differentiability allows end-to-end training of neural networks with physical simulation in the loop, bridging learned models with engineering-grade FEA without separating the optimization from the physics.
  - Sources: [Ansys FEM Background](https://www.ansys.com/simulation-topics/what-is-topology-optimization) · [Deep Energy Method](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.03072)

## Robotics

- **WholeBodyVLA: Unified Latent VLA for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation (ICLR 2026)**: WholeBodyVLA, published at ICLR 2026 by researchers from Fudan University and OpenDriveLab, presents a unified Vision-Language-Action framework that learns coordinated locomotion and manipulation from action-free egocentric videos using a Latent Action Model (LAM). The LAM encodes egocentric images and language instructions into latent action tokens decoded into simultaneous dual-arm joint commands and locomotion velocities—enabling end-to-end whole-body control for complex navigation-and-manipulation tasks. The key novelty is learning manipulation-aware locomotion: prior modular approaches treat walking and grasping independently, but WholeBodyVLA treats them as unified latent behaviors.

- **RoboPocket: Smartphone-Based Robot Policy Improvement Without a Physical Robot (arXiv March 2026)**: RoboPocket enables iterative robot policy improvement using only a consumer smartphone. A Remote Inference framework projects the robot's predicted action trajectory into the user's physical environment via Augmented Reality, letting annotators identify failures and correct trajectories without deploying a robot at all. An asynchronous online finetuning pipeline then updates the policy in real time. In experiments, only 12 AR corrections per scene improved success rates from 0.52 to 0.81, with 2x data efficiency over offline scaling strategies—suggesting that interactive trajectory annotation via AR is a scalable path to closing the robot data gap.
  - Sources: [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05504) · [Project Website](https://robo-pocket.github.io/) · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/papers/2603.05504)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **Bistable Origami Soft Pneumatic Actuators (BOSPA) for Multi-Modal Locomotion (2026)**: A new bistable origami-inspired soft pneumatic actuator has been published featuring a 3D-printed inflatable Miura-origami tube with two parallel air chambers and strategically placed elastic rings enabling snap-through bistability. By tuning chamber pressures, the actuator switches between three distinct deformation modes: linear extension, pure bending, and coupled bending/extension with snap-through transitions. This programmable multi-modality in a single actuator allows soft legged robots to switch gaits without reconfiguring hardware—a step toward soft robots with adaptive locomotion strategies.
  - Sources: [SAGE Journals BOSPA](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024) · [Actuators Journal Bi-OSPA](https://doi.org/10.3390/act15060320)

- **Bidirectional Origami Soft Pneumatic Actuator (Bi-OSPA): Extension and Contraction in One Chamber**: The Bi-OSPA achieves bidirectional motion—both elongation and contraction—within a single origami-structured air chamber, where the direction is governed entirely by the pressure sign: positive pressure extends, vacuum pressure contracts. This eliminates the need for opposing actuator pairs in antagonistic configurations, simplifying soft robotic limb design and reducing mass and complexity without sacrificing the ability to generate both pushing and pulling forces.
  - Sources: [Actuators Journal](https://doi.org/10.3390/act15060320) · [Science Robotics Origami Arm](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scirobotics.aar2915)

- **Geometry-Based Pneumatic Actuators (GPAs) Enable Wearable Exoskeleton and Haptic Interfaces (arXiv Feb 2026)**: GPAs use CNC heat-sealed flexible chambers whose geometry—not material stiffness—determines deformation mode, achieving near-zero minimum bending radii and multi-state actuation patterns. Mathematical modeling reveals predictable linear angle-pressure relationships and validated nonlinear torque-angle curves. In demonstrations: a 49-gram wrist exoskeleton reduced muscle activity by 51%; a 30.8-gram haptic interface delivered 8 N force feedback; and a 208-gram bipedal robot achieved multi-gait locomotion—all from the same actuator design paradigm.
  - Sources: [arXiv GPAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24104) · [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.24104v1)

- **Shape-Memory Polymer Origami Actuators for Variable-Stiffness Continuum Robots (ACS AMI 2026)**: 3D-printed shape-memory polymer (SMP) origami actuators achieve switchable stiffness states in continuum robot limbs, enabling dynamic deformation under actuation followed by structural locking for load bearing. An origami skeleton with SMP arc-strips at crease vertices allows reconfiguration via thermal stimulus, enabling a single limb design to occupy multiple stable configurations—solving the fundamental soft robot tradeoff between compliance during actuation and rigidity during loading.
  - Sources: [ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.5c04496) · [Origami Robotics Review](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/16/9/1047)

- **Origami Robotics in Biomedical Applications: A Review (Springer Nature 2026)**: A comprehensive review published in Annals of Biomedical Engineering catalogs origami-inspired robot designs across surgical tools, drug delivery systems, minimally invasive devices, and implantable structures. It identifies deployability (compact flat storage → expanded 3D function) as the primary property driving biomedical adoption, and highlights self-folding origami driven by shape-memory materials as the most promising pathway to untethered, implantable robotic structures.
  - Sources: [Springer Nature Review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10439-026-04078-w) · [MDPI Origami Robotics](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/16/9/1047)

- **Soft Pneumatic Actuator Review: Motion-Based Classification of Mechanisms and Trade-offs (arXiv May 2026)**: A comprehensive review paper proposes a motion-based taxonomy for soft pneumatic actuators, classifying designs by their primary deformation modes—bending, extension, contraction, twisting, and combinations—and analyzing trade-off surfaces between force output, bandwidth, stroke, and fabrication complexity. The review identifies a consistent finding: actuators optimized for one motion mode sacrifice performance on orthogonal modes, motivating the multistable and geometry-based designs seen elsewhere in 2026 literature.
  - Sources: [arXiv SPA Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25109) · [IEEE Morphological Design](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10296178/)

- **Anisotropic Stiffness Programmable Inflated Rotational Joints**: A design for inflatable rotational joints achieves programmable anisotropic stiffness—high resistance in some directions, low in others—through geometric control of inflation chamber topology. This enables soft robot links that are compliant during manipulation but rigid along load-bearing axes, without requiring active valving or variable-stiffness materials.
  - Sources: [arXiv Rotational Joint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13003)

## Biology & Medical Research

- **Daraxonrasib Nearly Doubles Survival in Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer (ASCO 2026, NEJM)**: Presented in the plenary session at ASCO 2026 in Chicago (May 31) and simultaneously published in the New England Journal of Medicine, daraxonrasib is a multi-selective RAS(ON) inhibitor—the first drug to successfully target mutated KRAS proteins, which drive more than 90% of pancreatic cancers.
