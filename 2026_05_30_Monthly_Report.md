## Computational Design

- **GenCAD (MIT): Image-to-Parametric-CAD via Transformer + Latent Diffusion**: MIT researchers introduced GenCAD, a generative model that transforms a single image into a complete, parameterized CAD command sequence — not a static mesh or point cloud. The architecture uses transformer-based contrastive representation to align the latent spaces of images and CAD programs, followed by a latent diffusion model to generate CAD command histories, which a geometry kernel then executes into editable 3D solids. This is the first system capable of recovering full design intent (sketches, extrusions, fillets) from a photograph.
  - Sources: [GenCAD Project Page](https://gencad.github.io/) · [AIToolly Coverage](https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-05-18-mit-researchers-introduce-gencad-a-generative-ai-model-for-image-conditioned-parametric-cad-program) · [ArXiv 2409.16294](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16294) · [Sesame Disk Summary](https://sesamedisk.com/gencad-image-conditioned-cad-generation/)

- **SOLIDWORKS 2026: AI Virtual Companions LEO and MARIE Embedded in Design**: Dassault Systèmes' SOLIDWORKS 2026 introduces two persistent AI Virtual Companions accessible via the 3DEXPERIENCE platform: LEO for mechanical engineering workflows, and MARIE for materials science, microbiology, and chemistry. Unlike one-shot AI tools, these function as continuous collaborative partners within the design session, marking a significant step toward normalizing generative AI in mainstream mechanical CAD.
  - Sources: [SOLIDWORKS AI Augmentation](https://www.solidworks.com/solution/how-ai-is-augmenting-cad-tools-better-product-design) · [Engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/3-ai-features-coming-to-every-cad-program-in-2026/)

## Computational Sciences

- **End-to-End Quantum Algorithm for Structural Topology Optimization**: Published in IOP Quantum Science and Technology (arxiv:2510.07280), a team from RWTH Aachen formulated structural topology optimization as a combinatorial satisfiability problem solved via Grover's algorithm on a fault-tolerant quantum computer. The stiffness matrix is block-encoded, compliance is computed via Quantum Singular Value Transformation and Quantum Amplitude Estimation, and the method evaluates exponentially many candidate structures in polynomial time — a quadratic speedup over classical unstructured search for this problem class.
  - Sources: [ArXiv 2510.07280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07280) · [IOPscience Publication](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4cfe) · [RWTH Publications](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/1033016)

- **SOPTX: Modular, Multi-Backend Topology Optimization Framework Released**: Published in Communications in Computational Physics, SOPTX provides a unified, extensible topology optimization codebase with support for multiple computational backends (CPU, GPU, and JAX). Designed as a research platform, it lowers the barrier to testing new TO algorithms and coupling them with physics solvers, addressing the longstanding fragmentation of the topology optimization software ecosystem.
  - Sources: [SOPTX Paper — Comm. in Comp. Physics](https://www.global-sci.com/cicp/article/view/24166)

- **Deep Reinforcement Learning + Genetic Algorithms + FEM for On-the-Fly Design**: A framework combining deep reinforcement learning (DRL), genetic algorithms (GA), and finite element methods (FEM) generates physics-simulation training data on the fly during agent training rather than requiring a pre-built dataset. Demonstrated on rocket nozzle design optimization, the approach avoids the dataset bias and coverage gaps of offline training — an important step toward general-purpose computational design agents that explore design spaces dynamically.
  - Sources: [Science Publishing Group — DRL + GA + FEM](https://www.sciencepublishinggroup.com/article/10.11648/j.ajasr.20261201.11)

- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks with Hard Constraints for Inverse Structural Design**: A SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing paper demonstrates PINNs that embed hard physical constraints — rather than soft penalty terms — for inverse design and topology optimization. The hard-constraint formulation guarantees constraint satisfaction throughout training, improving convergence and solution quality on canonical 2D topology optimization benchmarks compared to penalty-based PINN approaches.
  - Sources: [SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/21M1397908) · [ASTESJ Physics-Informed TO](https://www.astesj.com/v06/i04/p27/)

- **CompreSSM: Control-Theory-Based Compression of State-Space Models During Training**: MIT's CompreSSM technique applies control theory tools to identify which state dimensions in structured state-space models (like Mamba) contribute meaningfully to model outputs, then prunes them early in training. Applied to long-sequence models used in simulation and time-series prediction, the method yields leaner models without post-hoc accuracy loss — relevant for real-time physics simulations running on edge hardware.
  - Sources: [MIT News — CompreSSM](https://news.mit.edu/2026/new-technique-makes-ai-models-leaner-faster-while-still-learning-0409)

- **Quantum-Inspired Topology Optimization Sees Expanded Classical Implementation**: Beyond full quantum algorithms, researchers are applying quantum-inspired techniques — variational quantum eigensolver analogues, tensor network methods — to classical hardware for topology optimization. These hybrid approaches exploit quantum algorithmic structures without requiring fault-tolerant quantum hardware, providing near-term gains in solving high-resolution compliance minimization problems on classical GPUs.
  - Sources: [ArXiv Variational Quantum TO](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.07099) · [Wiley Quantum-Inspired TO Chapter](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119831839.ch9)

- **AI-Driven Lattice and Implicit Geometry Simulation Converges with Fabrication**: In 2026, the boundary between topology optimization, implicit geometry modeling, and manufacturing simulation is dissolving. Tools like nTopology now integrate direct simulation of additively manufactured lattices (including build-direction anisotropy and process-induced defects) as feedback within the generative design loop — turning simulation from a validation step into an embedded constraint during generation.

## Key Takeaways

- **Agentic AI is now baseline, not a feature**: GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Ultra, Claude Opus 4.6, and virtually every new frontier model ship agentic multi-step execution as a core design decision. The industry has crossed the threshold from AI-as-assistant to AI-as-autonomous-executor, with implications for every field from computational design (AI design agents) to robotics (VLA-driven robots). [WhatLLM May 2026](https://whatllm.org/blog/new-ai-models-may-2026) · [State of Robotics 2026](https://www.roboticscenter.ai/state-of-robotics-2026)

- **Origami + pneumatics is the dominant paradigm for soft robotic locomotion**: Three independent 2026 papers — BOSPA (Miura origami), Kresling crawling robot, and SOPA modular joints — all use bistable or foldable origami structures with pneumatic actuation to achieve speed, force, and motion versatility that quasi-static soft actuators cannot match. Bistability is the key enabling mechanism, converting slow pressure buildup into rapid, high-energy stroke release. [SAGE BOSPA](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024) · [IET Kresling](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/tje2.70159)

- **Personalized genomic medicine has moved from concept to clinical reality**: Baby KJ's treatment — a bespoke CRISPR base editor designed and delivered in six months — demonstrates that the infrastructure now exists to manufacture patient-specific genomic drugs at clinical timescales. Paired with AMT-130's 75% Huntington's slowdown and PCSK9 epigenetic silencing trials, gene therapy is transitioning from rare-disease niche to general medicine. [CHOP Baby KJ](https://www.chop.edu/news/childrens-hospital-philadelphia-marks-one-year-anniversary-worlds-first-personalized-crispr) · [Science AAAS AMT-130](https://www.science.org/content/article/first-gene-therapy-seems-slow-huntington-disease)

- **Metal AM has broken the 3-meter barrier — industrial-scale printing arrives**: Eplus3D's EP-M3050 (3050mm × 3050mm, 256 lasers) marks the moment when metal powder bed fusion becomes competitive with casting and forging for large structural components. Combined with the ~40% of AM output now going to end-use parts (up from a prototype-dominated market), additive manufacturing is completing its transition to production technology. [Eplus3D EP-M3050](https://www.eplus3d.com/eplus3d-metal-additive-manufacturing-EP-M3050-.html) · [PatSnap Generative Design for AM](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/rd-blog/generative-design-for-additive-manufacturing-2026/)

- **CAD is being redesigned from scratch around AI-native workflows**: GenCAD (image → parametric history), Autodesk Neural CAD (text → editable geometry), and SOLIDWORKS' embedded AI companions LEO and MARIE collectively represent a redesign of the CAD paradigm — from geometry authoring to geometry retrieval and refinement from AI-generated candidates. The parametric history (not just shape) is now the target output. [GenCAD ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16294) · [Engineering.com CAD AI](https://www.engineering.com/3-ai-features-coming-to-every-cad-program-in-2026/)

- **Multimaterial 3D printing is converging with functional materials science**: Harvard's rotational printing embeds actuation; the PEDOT:PSS/CNT 2PP study prints conductivity and transparency; sequential multi-printer fabrication combines shape-memory polymers with hydrogels. These advances share a common pattern: functional material properties are becoming design parameters encoded directly into the print path, blurring the line between manufacturing and materials engineering. [Harvard PNAS](https://seas.harvard.edu/news/3d-printing-soft-robots) · [Advanced Science 2PP](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202513208?af=R)

- **Quantum algorithms for topology optimization are graduating from theory**: The RWTH Aachen end-to-end quantum algorithm for structural TO (arxiv:2510.07280) demonstrates a full fault-tolerant pipeline — block-encoded stiffness matrices, QSVT for solving linear systems, Grover search — with formal complexity guarantees. While hardware capable of running it at scale is still years away, having a complete algorithmic blueprint with proven quantum advantage is the prerequisite for hardware investment in this domain. [ArXiv 2510.07280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07280) · [IOPscience](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4cfe)
