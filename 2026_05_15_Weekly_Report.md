## 3D Printing / Additive Manufacturing

- **Eplus3D EP-M3050 Breaks the 3-Meter Barrier**: Eplus3D announced the EP-M3050, a metal powder bed fusion system with a 3,050 × 3,050 mm build area, up to 256 lasers, and a configurable Z-axis up to 5,000 mm — enabling one-piece production of aerospace and energy components at previously impossible scales, with theoretical print speeds of up to 3,500 cm³/h. [Source](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/eplus3d-shatters-the-three-meter-ceiling-in-metal-powder-bed-fusion-250915/)

- **ORNL Demonstrates AM for Hot Isostatic Pressing Canisters**: Oak Ridge National Laboratory showed that additive manufacturing can fabricate custom canisters for powder metallurgical hot isostatic pressing, cutting manufacturing steps, material waste, and production time while enabling advanced alloy compositions not achievable through conventional forming. [Source](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-05-3d-enables-powder-metallurgical-hot.html)

- **Biochar from Agricultural Waste Improves Polymer Printing**: Researchers demonstrated that adding biochar — a carbon-rich byproduct of agricultural waste — to common FDM polymers (ABS, PLA, PP, PETG, HDPE) improves mechanical performance and sustainability metrics simultaneously, opening a new pathway for circular-economy materials in desktop and industrial printing. [Source](https://3dprint.com/325763/3d-printing-news-briefs-may-2-2026/)

- **America Makes Launches $25.6M MIAMI Defense Initiative**: America Makes and NCDMM announced two new Project Calls totaling $25.6M; the MIAMI initiative ($12.4M) focuses on validating metallic AM materials as drop-in replacements for traditional alloys in weapon system components. [Source](https://3dprint.com/325991/3d-printing-news-briefs-5-14-2026/)

- **Hot-Wire Laser Technique Preserves Cemented Carbide Hardness**: A new hot-wire laser approach softens tungsten carbide-cobalt (cemented carbide) rather than fully melting it during additive manufacturing, successfully preserving the material's extreme hardness and strength — a breakthrough for wear-resistant tooling printed via AM. [Source](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/03/260313002642.htm)

## Computational Sciences

- **Hybrid DRL–GA Topology Optimization Outperforms Standalone Methods**: A new framework published in the *American Journal of Applied Scientific Research* integrates deep reinforcement learning, genetic algorithms, and finite element methods for structural topology optimization, with the hybrid DRL–GA approach consistently outperforming standalone DRL by leveraging complementary strengths of exploration and evolutionary search. - **RL-Based TO for Generative Lightweight Structures**: Researchers published an RL-based topology optimization workflow specifically targeting lightweight structural design, demonstrating generative design pipelines that learn optimal material layouts via Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) within defined design spaces. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2215016125003838)

- **PSRS and DATTE: New Network Topology Optimization via DRL + GNNs**: Two new methods — Path Selection with Rejection Strategy (PSRS) and Dual-Agent Tree Topology Exploration (DATTE) — combine deep reinforcement learning with graph neural networks for network topology optimization, published in IEEE and extending DRL-based design to graph-structured problems. [Source](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11000124/)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **LEGO-like Origami Robots Standardize Soft Robot Design** *(Advanced Science, 2026)*: A new design framework treats soft robots as assemblies of off-the-shelf origami actuator modules, akin to LEGO bricks, achieving robot arm tip accuracy within 3 mm over a 400 mm span — a significant step toward modular, replicable soft robotic platforms. [Source](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202513881)

- **Bistable Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuators (BOSPA)**: A bistable origami-inspired pneumatic actuator based on a 3D-printed Miura-origami tube with dual air chambers achieves multiple deformation modes — linear extension, bending, and coupled bending/extension — with snap-through bistability transitions enabled by programmable driving pressures. [Source](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024)

- **Digital Actuation Control via Liquid Crystal Elastomer Hinges** *(Adv. Functional Materials, 2026)*: A design-for-manufacturing approach uses reversible shape-memory liquid crystal elastomer hinges with Joule heating to achieve fully reconfigurable, reversible, closed-loop digital actuation in self-folding origami robots — eliminating external pneumatic infrastructure. [Source](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adfm.202525150)

- **Origami Exoskeletons for Enhanced Soft Robotic Manipulation** *(Science Advances, 2026)*: Researchers published a study in *Science Advances* demonstrating origami-inspired exoskeletons that augment soft robotic manipulators, providing variable stiffness and improved grasping force without sacrificing the inherent compliance of soft actuators. [Source](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adv6629)

- **Meta-Ori: Nonlinear Inflatable Actuators via Metashell + Kresling Origami**: The Meta-Ori framework combines nonlinear cylindrical mechanical metamaterials (metashells) with Kresling origami inflatables into a monolithic actuator, enabling actuation sequencing from a single pressure input by tuning segment stiffness through print infill variation. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.23375v1)

## Key Takeaways

- **Agentic AI is the defining shift of 2026**: GPT-5.5, Gemini Intelligence, Claude Code, and Microsoft Agent 365 all mark the same transition — AI is no longer responding to queries but autonomously executing multi-step tasks across tools and systems, with enterprise infrastructure (Anthropic's $900B round, $25B OpenAI ARR) reflecting the monetization of this shift.

- **Metal 3D printing has broken its scale ceiling**: Eplus3D's EP-M3050 with its 256-laser, 3050 × 3050 mm build area shatters the long-standing three-meter barrier, unlocking single-build production of aerospace and energy structures previously impossible via additive methods.

- **Humanoid robotics has reached commercial pluralism**: The jump from 3 to 12 commercial humanoid platforms in two years — combined with VLA model adoption in 40% of deployments and the NVIDIA Jetson T4000 providing 4× more onboard compute — signals the industry has crossed from prototype to deployment phase.

- **Soft robotics is converging on modular, programmable platforms**: The LEGO-like origami framework, bistable pneumatic actuators, and LCE-hinge self-folding robots all point toward a future where compliant mechanisms are assembled from standardized, digitally controlled building blocks rather than custom-fabricated monoliths.

- **Gene therapy and targeted oncology are entering rapid clinical translation**: The FDA's new pathway for personalized CRISPR therapies, the Beqalzi BCL-2 inhibitor approval, and the validated amyloid pathway in Alzheimer's collectively indicate that genetic and molecular medicine is shifting from proof-of-concept to scalable patient access.
