## Computational Design

- **Mamba-CAD: State Space Models for Parametric CAD Generation**: Published at AAAI 2026, Mamba-CAD applies Mamba (a discrete State Space Model architecture) to generate parametric CAD sequences for complex industrial components. The model uses an encoder-decoder framework with a CAD reconstruction pre-training task, then guides a generative adversarial network to produce valid parametric sequences. A new dataset of 77,078 CAD models with longer-than-typical sequences was released with the work, and experiments demonstrated superior performance on valid sequence generation length—a key bottleneck for industrial-complexity parts.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2603.00439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00439) · [AAAI Proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32531) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390716842_Mamba-CAD_State_Space_Model_for_3D_Computer-Aided_Design_Generative_Modeling)

- **ReCAD: RL-Enhanced Parametric CAD from Vision-Language Models**: Also published at AAAI 2026, ReCAD uses reinforcement learning to bootstrap pretrained VLMs into generating precise parametric CAD from multimodal inputs. Rather than relying on fully supervised fine-tuning, the RL reward signal allows complex operations like pattern replication and mirroring to emerge from simple primitive interfaces, exploiting the generative priors already latent in large VLMs. The framework supports editability that previous SFT-only approaches lacked, making it more practical for iterative industrial design.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2512.06328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06328) · [AAAI Proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/37544) · [arXiv HTML version](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06328v1)

- **AI for Generative Additive Manufacturing — Agentic Era Review**: A comprehensive review in ScienceDirect traces the evolution of AI-driven generative design for additive manufacturing through four eras: geometry-centric GANs/VAEs (pre-2020), process-aware generation (2021–22), diffusion-model-based 3D generation (2023–24), and the current "agentic and physics-informed" phase. In this newest phase, LLMs integrated with RAG perform autonomous, knowledge-grounded workflow reasoning across the full design-to-print pipeline. Publication volume in 2025–2026 alone nearly matches all prior years combined.
  - Sources: [ScienceDirect — Generative AI for Additive Manufacturing](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0278612526000749) · [MeshMayhem Guide 2026](https://www.meshmayhem.com/post/ai-generated-parametric-models-the-future-of-cad-and-3d-printing-2026-guide)

- **Machine Learning Inverse Design for Metallic Mechanical Metamaterials**: A paper in Additive Manufacturing (Taylor & Francis, 2026) demonstrated fabrication-aware ML inverse design for metallic metamaterials, disentangling the complex interplay of digital architecture, AM process parameters, and as-built defects. An engineer specifies a target stiffness, deformation pathway, or energy absorption profile and the DL model maps it to a manufacturable microstructure with ~90% accuracy under experimental validation. The approach addresses long-standing challenges in scaling metamaterial design to arbitrary functional targets.
  - Sources: [Taylor & Francis — Additive Manufacturing](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17452759.2026.2668249) · [ScienceDirect — AI-Driven Metamaterial Design](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590048X26000701) · [Nature — Inverse Design with Geometric AI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44455-025-00005-6)

They enable interactive, natural-language-driven collaboration within the CAD workflow, supporting tasks ranging from constraint setting to drawing generation. The integration represents a shift from AI as a background optimization tool to AI as an active design collaborator operating within the CAD environment in real time.

- **Self-Supervised AI for Decoding Disordered Metamaterials**: Published in Science Advances, this work introduces a self-supervised approach for both decoding properties of disordered (non-periodic) metamaterials and designing new ones with targeted behaviors. Disordered metamaterials have historically resisted systematic design because their property-structure maps are high-dimensional and discontinuous. The self-supervised method learns the latent structure of these maps without labeled training data, enabling inverse design in a regime previously inaccessible to supervised ML.

## Computational Sciences

- **First Successful 3D PINN Simulation of M6 Wing Flow**: A significant milestone for industrial-complexity PDE solving was achieved with the first reported successful Physics-Informed Neural Network simulation of three-dimensional flow around the M6 transonic wing at Reynolds number 5,000. This case had long been a benchmark challenge for traditional CFD, and its successful solution by a PINN demonstrates that neural PDE solvers can now handle genuinely complex 3D aerodynamics without relying on structured meshes or iterative linear solvers. The result bolsters confidence that PINNs can eventually complement or replace traditional FEM/FVM in industrial design loops.
  - Sources: [Articsledge — PINNs Guide 2026](https://www.articsledge.com/post/physics-informed-neural-networks-pinns) · [arXiv — PINNs and Neural Operators](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04576) · [MDPI Applied Sciences — PINNs Review](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/14/8092)

- **GeoPT: Pre-Training Physics Simulation on Geometry Alone**: A recent paper introduces GeoPT, which achieves scalable pre-training for physics simulation by training on off-the-shelf geometry data rather than compute-expensive simulation outputs. This decouples the data requirements for foundation model pre-training from simulation cost—enabling training on millions of geometric shapes without running a single finite-element solve. The resulting model can then be fine-tuned on modest simulation datasets to generalize across physics regimes, representing a potentially transformative shift in how physics AI models are trained.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2602.20399 — GeoPT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20399) · [arXiv — Towards a Physics Foundation Model](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.13805)

- **PDEformer-2: Versatile Foundation Model for 2D PDEs**: PDEformer-2 is a transformer-based foundation model designed to solve arbitrary two-dimensional PDEs from symbolic descriptions of the governing equations and boundary conditions. Unlike single-physics neural operators, it processes the PDE structure as a token sequence alongside spatial input, enabling zero-shot generalization to new equation types. This modular approach could unify the fragmented landscape of physics-specific neural solvers and make simulation accessible to users who cannot afford bespoke model training.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2507.15409 — PDEformer-2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.15409) · [arXiv — Neural Operators for Scientific Simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15325)

- **PINN-FDM Coupling for Thermal-Hydraulic Systems**: A recent preprint presented a numerical method for coupling parameterized PINNs with Finite Difference Methods (FDM) for advanced thermal-hydraulic system simulation. The hybrid approach leverages the meshless extrapolation of PINNs in regions of high geometric complexity while retaining FDM in structured bulk regions, achieving better accuracy-per-compute than pure PINN or pure FDM approaches. This hybrid paradigm may become a practical workaround for the scaling limitations of monolithic PINN approaches on complex industrial geometries.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2604.02663](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.02663) · [arXiv — Evo-PINN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.06572)

- **Topology Optimization Special Issue at MDPI Mathematics**: The journal Mathematics (MDPI) has an active special issue on "Advances in Topology Optimization for Advanced Manufacturing" with a manuscript submission deadline of June 30, 2026, indicating a concentrated moment of publication in this area. Key themes include physics-informed deep learning for TO, implicit neural representation (INR)-based TO enabling arbitrary-resolution sampling, and GPU-accelerated pseudo-transient methods (PeTTO). INR-based approaches in particular enable the topology optimizer to produce continuous, resolution-independent material distributions rather than fixed-grid binary results.
  - Sources: [MDPI Mathematics Special Issue](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/mathematics/special_issues/5T51YRF85B) · [arXiv:2509.06971 — PeTTO](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.06971)

- **Machine Learning Inverse Design for Bimaterial Thermoelastic Lattice Metamaterials**: A 2026 preprint demonstrates ML-enabled inverse design for bimaterial thermoelastic lattice structures, solving the challenge of designing architected materials that exhibit prescribed thermomechanical coupling behaviors. The design space is high-dimensional because both constituent materials and spatial arrangement interact nonlinearly. The learned inverse map reduces a problem that would require millions of finite-element evaluations to a forward pass through a trained network, with immediate relevance for actuator design and shape-morphing structures.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2602.20173 — Bimaterial Thermoelastic Lattice ML](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20173) · [Wiley Advanced Functional Materials — ML Inverse Design](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adfm.75070)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **Bistable Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuators (BOSPA) with Multiple Deformation Modes**: Published in June 2026 (Sage Journals), this work introduces the Bistable Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuator (BOSPA) for soft-legged locomotion. The actuator consists of a 3D-printed inflatable Miura-origami tube structure with two parallel air chambers and strategically placed elastic rings that enable bistability. By programming the driving air pressure, the actuator achieves multiple deformation modes—linear extension, bending, and coupled bending/extension—as well as amplified output force and rapid snap-through transitions between states, enabling dynamic gaits in soft-legged robots.
  - Sources: [SAGE Journals — BOSPA](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024)

- **Bidirectional Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuator**: A June 2026 publication in the journal Actuators (MDPI) presented a bidirectional origami-inspired soft pneumatic actuator design addressing the challenge of bidirectional motion in a compact and analytically tractable architecture. The design leverages origami folding kinematics to describe deformation primarily through crease patterns rather than material elasticity, enabling an analytical model that relates input pressure to output displacement and force. Bidirectional capability within a single actuator without antagonistic chambers simplifies the pneumatic circuit and reduces system weight.
  - Sources: [MDPI Actuators — Bidirectional Origami](https://doi.org/10.3390/act15060320)

- **Rotational Multi-Material Printing of Soft Robots with Embedded Pneumatics**: Harvard researchers (Advanced Materials, 2026) demonstrated that controlling print nozzle rotation rate, extrusion rate, and path simultaneously enables programmable embedded pneumatic channels in single-print elastomeric soft robots. The technique produces asymmetric core-shell filaments in 1D, 2D, and 2.5D spatial motifs, and by removing the fugitive ink channel, creates pneumatic routing that encodes the actuation behavior geometrically. The Fermat spiral path planning algorithm enables automatic generation of complex robots (e.g., hand-inspired grippers) without manual channel routing.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2505.18095](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18095) · [Fabbaloo](https://www.fabbaloo.com/news/rotational-multimaterial-3d-printing-of-pneumatically-programmable-soft-robots) · [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-02-rotating-nozzle-3d-air-powered.html)

- **Shape-Memory Polymer Origami for Multimodal Soft Continuum Robots**: Published in ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces (2026), this work integrates shape-memory polymer (SMP) variable-stiffness hinges into 3D-printed origami-inspired modules for soft continuum robots. The SMP hinges exploit the glass transition to seamlessly switch between soft (compliant, deformable) and stiff (load-bearing, locked) states, enabling robots that can perform delicate manipulation in soft mode and structural load-bearing in stiff mode. The continuum robot architecture allows the modules to be assembled into configurations with varying curvature and reach.
  - Sources: [ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces — SMP Origami](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.5c04496) · [MDPI Machines — Morph and Function](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/16/9/1047)

- **Monolithic 3D Printing of Origami Soft Robots from Sustainable Bio-Based Resin**: A 2026 paper (PMC) demonstrated monolithic (single-step) fabrication of origami-inspired soft robots using a fully bio-based photopolymer resin, eliminating the multi-step molding processes and petroleum-derived elastomers that dominate current soft robotics fabrication. The bio-based resin retains the elasticity and fatigue resistance required for repeated folding and inflation cycles. This represents an important step toward sustainable soft robotics manufacturing where the full lifecycle—from material sourcing to end-of-life—is considered.
  - Sources: [PMC — Monolithic Bio-Based Origami Soft Robots](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13042658/) · [Geometry-Based Pneumatic Actuators](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.24104)

- **Programmable Deformation via Volumetric-Pattern-Induced Anisotropy**: A preprint introduced a design method for porous soft actuators in which programmable deformation is encoded through spatial patterning of volumetric anisotropy within the printed structure rather than through channel geometry or material composition. By varying pore orientation and density in a 3D lattice, the resulting actuator bends, twists, or extends in prescribed ways upon uniform pressure input. The approach decouples the actuation pathway from the pneumatic circuit, potentially simplifying fabrication while expanding the repertoire of achievable motions.
  - Sources: [arXiv:2512.12320 — Porous Soft Actuator Anisotropy](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.12320) · [arXiv — Inflated Rotational Joint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13003)

## Biology & Medical Research

- **VIPR System: Gene Silencing Without DNA Cutting**: A newly discovered molecular system called VIPR (Versatile Inhibitory Programmable Repressor) silences target genes without making double-strand DNA breaks, using a unique noncontiguous recognition code. At only ~180 amino acids, VIPR is small enough to be packaged into adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors—the most clinically validated gene therapy delivery vehicle. The approach opens a new therapeutic modality combining the precision of programmable nucleic acid targeting with the safety profile of non-cutting mechanisms, with potential applications across any disease driven by gene overexpression.
  - Sources: [Works in Progress — What's New in Biology June 2026](https://www.worksinprogress.news/p/whats-new-in-biology-june-2026) · [ScienceDaily — CRISPR turns genes on without cutting](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/01/260104202813.htm)

- **First Oral COVID Post-Exposure Prophylactic Passes Phase 3**: The first oral small-molecule drug to prevent COVID-19 after exposure succeeded in a Phase 3 clinical trial, representing a major advance in antiviral medicine. Unlike vaccines (which require weeks for immunity) or monoclonal antibodies (which require infusion), an oral prophylactic can be dispensed at pharmacy counter-speed and self-administered. If approved, it would provide a new tool for outbreak control, immunocompromised individuals, and settings where vaccine access is limited.
  - Sources: [Works in Progress — What's New in Biology June 2026](https://www.worksinprogress.news/p/whats-new-in-biology-june-2026)

- **Personalized CRISPR Therapy for Infant with CPS1 Deficiency**: A team at Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP) and Penn Medicine treated a child born with carbamoyl phosphate synthetase 1 (CPS1) deficiency—a rare, often fatal metabolic disorder—using a bespoke CRISPR gene editing therapy designed specifically for the infant's mutation. First administered in February 2025, the infant received the therapy safely and is now growing and thriving. This case establishes proof of concept for truly personalized gene therapies engineered for individual patients' mutations rather than population-level genetic variants.
  - Sources: [CHOP — World's First Personalized CRISPR Therapy](https://www.chop.edu/news/worlds-first-patient-treated-personalized-crispr-gene-editing-therapy-childrens-hospital) · [FBAE — CRISPR Clinical Trials 2026](https://www.fbae.org/blog/crispr-clinical-trials-2026/) · [PMC — Advancing CRISPR into Clinical Trials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12094669/)

- **CRISPR Restores Chemotherapy Sensitivity in Head and Neck Cancer**: Researchers at ChristianaCare's Gene Editing Institute used CRISPR to restore chemotherapy sensitivity in head and neck tumor cells that had developed resistance, representing a novel application of gene editing to overcome treatment failure rather than directly correcting a monogenic disease. Chemotherapy resistance is responsible for the majority of cancer-related deaths, making approaches that re-sensitize refractory tumors clinically significant. The work suggests CRISPR could eventually become a precision tool in oncology for combatting acquired drug resistance.
  - Sources: [ChristianaCare — CRISPR Head/Neck Cancer](https://news.christianacare.org/2026/01/gene-editing-breakthrough-offers-new-hope-for-head-and-neck-cancer-patients/) · [PMC — Advancing CRISPR into Clinical Trials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12094669/)

- **Stereo-Cell: Spatial Single-Cell Multiomics at Subcellular Resolution**: Published in Science, Stereo-cell is a spatial single-cell sequencing platform built on high-density DNA nanoball (DNB)-patterned arrays that simultaneously profiles transcriptomics and proteomics while preserving spatial tissue context. The system profiled ~445,000 cells in a single run, detected rare hematopoietic stem cells at ~0.05% frequency consistent with known biology, and resolved subcellular transcript localization in structurally complex samples like multinucleated skeletal myofibers. By bridging advanced omics with traditional histopathology, Stereo-cell could enable clinically interpretable spatial diagnoses at single-cell resolution.
  - Sources: [Science — Stereo-cell](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adr0475) · [PMC — Stereo-cell clinical translation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12455014/) · [AGBT 2026 Genomics Coverage](https://cen.acs.org/analytical-chemistry/sequencing/agbt-2026-dna-sequencing-multiomics-spatial/104/web/2026/03)

- **AI Revolution in Transcriptomics — From Single Cells to Spatial Atlases**: A comprehensive review in Advanced Science (Wiley, 2026) charted how AI is transforming transcriptomics across scales: from denoising and batch-correction of single-cell RNA-seq to integrating multi-tissue spatial atlases into coherent maps of human biology. Foundation models pre-trained on millions of single-cell transcriptomes are now enabling zero-shot cell-type annotation and cross-study generalization, tasks that previously required extensive manual curation. The review identifies spatial transcriptomics + AI as the next major frontier for understanding organ-level cell communication.
  - Sources: [Advanced Science — AI Revolution in Transcriptomics](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202518949) · [Frontiers — Single-cell RNA-seq advances](https://www.frontierspartnerships.org/journals/acta-biochimica-polonica/articles/10.3389/abp.2025.13922/full)

- **NIH Funding Cuts of $9.5B Disrupt U.S. Biomedical Research Pipeline**: The current U.S. administration cut approximately $9.5 billion in NIH research funding by June 2026, accompanied by roughly 20,000 job reductions across the FDA, CDC, and NIH. Regulatory review timelines have lengthened and several clinical programs—including Sensorion's gene therapy for OTOF-related hearing loss—have been cancelled citing adverse changes in the development environment. The cuts represent the largest single-year reduction in U.S. federal biomedical research funding in recorded history and are expected to suppress domestic research output for years.

- **Physical AI reaches commercial deployment**: NVIDIA's GR00T N1.7 becoming the first commercially licensed generalist robot foundation model—alongside the open reference humanoid hardware—signals that physical AI has crossed from research into product. Combined with Boston Dynamics' Atlas entering production and Automate 2026's first Humanoid Pavilion, June 2026 may mark the start of the commercial humanoid era. [GR00T N1.7](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-releases-new-physical-ai-models-as-global-partners-unveil-next-generation-robots) · [Boston Dynamics Atlas](https://www.automate.org/robotics/industry-insights/boston-dynamics-to-begin-production-on-redesigned-atlas-humanoid-in-2026)

- **AI closes the loop on generative design**: Mamba-CAD and ReCAD (both AAAI 2026) represent a qualitative advance in AI-native parametric CAD generation—moving from geometry prediction to full parametric sequence generation with editability. Paired with ML inverse design for metamaterials achieving ~90% experimental accuracy, AI is transitioning from design assistant to primary designer in fabrication workflows. [Mamba-CAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00439) · [ReCAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06328) · [ML Metamaterial Inverse Design](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17452759.2026.2668249)

- **Origami + pneumatics matures as a fabrication paradigm**: Three distinct June 2026 publications (BOSPA bistable actuators, bidirectional origami actuators, and Harvard's rotational multi-material embedded pneumatics) reflect the maturation of origami-inspired pneumatic robotics from individual research demonstrations to a systematic design and fabrication methodology. The convergence of analytical modeling, printable geometries, and multi-material AM is creating a coherent toolbox for programmable soft robotic matter. [BOSPA](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024) · [Harvard Embedded Pneumatics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18095)

- **Gene editing beyond the cut**: The VIPR system (gene silencing without DNA cutting), CRISPR-based chemotherapy sensitivity restoration, and the first personalized CRISPR therapy for an infant all appeared within the same reporting period—indicating that the gene therapy field is rapidly expanding both its toolbox and its therapeutic reach. The shift toward non-cutting mechanisms (base editing, prime editing, VIPR, CRISPRi) could substantially reduce the safety barriers that have limited broader clinical adoption. [VIPR](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/01/260104202813.htm) · [Personalized CRISPR](https://www.chop.edu/news/worlds-first-patient-treated-personalized-crispr-gene-editing-therapy-childrens-hospital)

- **Physics simulation at an inflection point**: GeoPT's insight that geometry alone—without simulation data—can drive meaningful pre-training for physics foundation models, combined with PDEformer-2's zero-shot generalization across 2D PDE types and the first successful 3D PINN aerodynamics simulation, suggest neural simulation is transitioning from narrow specialist tools to general-purpose physics engines. This could collapse simulation setup time from days to minutes for a wide range of engineering domains. [GeoPT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.20399) · [PDEformer-2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.15409)

- **Spatial biology + AI as the next organ-level map**: Stereo-cell's ability to profile 445,000 cells per run with simultaneous RNA and protein at subcellular resolution, combined with the Advanced Science review on AI-driven transcriptomics, points to a near-term inflection where whole-organ cell atlases become clinically actionable rather than purely descriptive. The integration of spatial context with multiomics is the missing link between bench-scale biology and tissue-level disease understanding. [Stereo-cell](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adr0475) · [AI in Transcriptomics](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202518949)

- **U.S. biomedical and AI infrastructure diverge sharply**: While private AI investment is driving rapid capability advances (Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini Omni, GR00T N2), the $9.5B NIH funding cut and FDA/CDC workforce reductions represent a historic withdrawal of public investment in basic research and regulatory science. The divergence creates a structural risk: frontier AI capabilities may advance faster than the public biomedical research infrastructure needed to validate and safely translate them into medicine.
