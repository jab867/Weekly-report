[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/harvard-researchers-develop-novel-3d-printing-method-for-soft-robotics-rotational-multi-material-method-creates-muscle-like-structures-that-can-be-programmed-to-twist-lift-or-bend)

- **Princeton Electric Soft-Rigid Hybrid Robots**: Princeton researchers combined 3D-printed liquid crystal elastomers, flexible electronics, and origami-folding techniques to build soft-rigid hybrid robots driven by targeted electric current alone — no external pneumatics or motors required. [Source](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1123376)

- **3D Printed Custom Surgical Plates Research**: Durham University's Dr. Alessandro Borghi received £125,000 to optimize stiffness and geometry of 3D printed facial reconstruction surgical plates using computer simulations to improve stress distribution and long-term healing outcomes. - **Biochar-Enhanced Printing Polymers**: Researchers demonstrated that adding biochar derived from agricultural waste to common 3D printing polymers (ABS, PLA, PETG, PP, HDPE) improves dimensional accuracy, reduces porosity, and increases tensile strength, offering a sustainable route to improved FDM performance. ## Computational Sciences

- **Deep RL + Genetic Algorithm Topology Optimization**: A new paper in the *American Journal of Applied Scientific Research* presents a hybrid framework combining deep reinforcement learning (DRL), genetic algorithms (GA), and finite element methods (FEM) for topology optimization, with the hybrid DRL–GA method consistently outperforming standalone DRL across benchmark problems. - **Comprehensive Review of Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Structural Engineering**: A major review in *Artificial Intelligence Review* (Springer) surveys PINNs across structural engineering applications, cataloging architectures, constraint-handling strategies, and open research gaps relevant to inverse design and simulation. - **Advanced Topology Optimization Methods Review**: An MDPI *Computation* special issue on advanced topology optimization methods and applications reviews the current state of the art, covering manufacturing constraints, multi-physics coupling, additive manufacturing design, and large-scale solvers. [Source](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-3197/14/2/29)

- **Neural Network-Assisted GA for Production System Topology**: A *Computer-Integrated Manufacturing* paper introduces a simulation-based optimization workflow pairing a neural network surrogate with a genetic algorithm to optimize production system topology — demonstrating how ML surrogates can accelerate expensive simulation-in-the-loop design problems. [Source](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0951192X.2025.2455631)

## Key Takeaways

- **AI is entering physical and geometric domains**: From NASA's AI-planned Mars rover drives to Autodesk Neural CAD generating editable parametric geometry from text, AI is moving decisively from language into 3D space, fabrication, and physical-world autonomy.
- **Soft robotics and additive manufacturing are converging**: Harvard's rotational multimaterial printing, Princeton's electric origami robots, and the BOSPA actuator all demonstrate that advanced fabrication is now the core enabling technology for compliant, functional soft machines.
- **NVIDIA's Physical AI stack is production-ready**: The simultaneous GA release of GR00T N1.7, Isaac Sim 6.0, Isaac Lab 3.0, and Newton 1.0 — plus the Hugging Face integration — signals that the infrastructure for training and deploying general-purpose robots has reached a deployable state.
- **CRISPR is transitioning from research to clinical reality**: Miniaturized CRISPR for AAV delivery, epigenetic activation without DNA cuts, and NRF2-targeting cancer applications represent a maturation of the toolset; the 2026 Breakthrough Prize reflects how transformative the prior decade of gene editing work has been.
- **Topology optimization is being reshaped by RL and hybrid ML methods**: Multiple 2026 publications show DRL + GA hybrid methods, PPO-based structural optimization, and PINN-driven inverse design converging on fabrication-aware, constraint-satisfying geometry generation — directly relevant to additive manufacturing design pipelines.
