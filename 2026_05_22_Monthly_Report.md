## Artificial Intelligence

- **AlphaEvolve — Gemini-Powered Evolutionary Algorithm Discovery (Google DeepMind, May 2026)**: AlphaEvolve pairs Gemini's generative coding capabilities with automated evaluators and an evolutionary loop that selects and improves the most promising algorithm candidates. Its most striking result: the first improvement in 56 years over Strassen's matrix multiplication algorithm, reducing 4×4 complex-valued matrix multiplication from 49 to 48 scalar multiplications. Across a broad benchmark suite, AlphaEvolve replicated known optimal solutions 75% of the time and found previously unknown optima in 20% of cases. Beyond pure mathematics, the system has been applied to optimize Google's own data centers, chip design pipelines, and AI training schedules, yielding millions in compute savings.

## Computational Design

- **MIT GenCAD — Image-Conditioned Parametric CAD Generation (May 18, 2026)**: GenCAD is a generative model that takes an image as input and outputs a complete parametric CAD command history — a sequence of construction operations rather than a static mesh or point cloud. Its architecture combines transformer-based contrastive image-to-shape representation with latent diffusion priors over the command sequence space. Because the output is the CAD program itself, the resulting model is fully editable: engineers can modify individual feature dimensions, constraints, or operations after generation. This addresses a long-standing gap in AI-to-CAD pipelines, where prior methods produced geometry unsuitable for downstream engineering workflows.
  - Sources: [AIToolly](https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-05-18-mit-researchers-introduce-gencad-a-generative-ai-model-for-image-conditioned-parametric-cad-program) · [Sesame Disk](https://sesamedisk.com/gencad-image-conditioned-cad-generation/) · [GenCAD Project](https://gencad.github.io/)

The release normalizes generative AI in everyday engineering workflows at enterprise scale.

## Computational Sciences

- **SOPTX — High-Performance Multi-Backend Topology Optimization Framework (arXiv May 2025 / CiCP 2026)**: SOPTX is an open-source framework built on FEALPy that decouples structural analysis from optimization in a non-intrusive architecture, supporting NumPy, PyTorch, and JAX computational backends. CPU and GPU execution are unified through the same API, and automatic differentiation handles sensitivity computation, eliminating the need to hand-derive adjoints. Fast matrix assembly overcomes the performance bottleneck of traditional numerical integration, and the modular design supports arbitrary problem dimensions and mesh types. Numerical benchmarks demonstrate leading efficiency in both computational speed and memory usage for compliance minimization with volume constraints.
  - Sources: [arXiv 2505.02438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02438) · [CiCP Journal](https://www.global-sci.com/cicp/article/view/24166)

- **AlphaEvolve Applied to Open Mathematical Problems (Google DeepMind, 2026)**: Beyond its matrix multiplication result, AlphaEvolve improved the known bound on the kissing number problem (how many non-overlapping unit spheres can simultaneously touch a central sphere in high dimensions) — one of the long-standing open questions in combinatorial geometry. The evolutionary search strategy, which maintains a population of candidate algorithms and repeatedly mutates and evaluates them, is general-purpose and has been applied to packing, sorting, hashing, and scheduling problems. Its ability to discover novel solutions rather than interpolating from training data represents a qualitative shift from large-language-model code generation.
  - Sources: [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/deepmind-alphaevolve) · [DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/) · [Wikipedia AlphaEvolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaEvolve)

- **AI Surrogate for Nonlinear Optics Simulations (Stanford/UCLA/SLAC, May 2026)**: A deep learning surrogate model, jointly developed by Stanford University, UCLA, and SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, achieves orders-of-magnitude acceleration over conventional nonlinear optics simulation while maintaining high fidelity across a wide range of pulse shapes. By mapping pulse input parameters directly to output spectra, the surrogate enables real-time optimization of ultrafast laser systems and large-scale parametric sweeps that were previously computationally prohibitive.
  - Sources: [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-ai-surrogate-nonlinear-optics-simulations.html)

- **Quantum Algorithm for Topology Optimization (arXiv, 2025)**: An end-to-end quantum algorithm for topology optimization evaluates the structural compliance of exponentially many candidate designs in quantum superposition, maintaining Grover's quadratic speedup over classical exhaustive search. The approach encodes material distributions as quantum states and uses quantum amplitude estimation to extract structural performance metrics. While full-scale hardware implementation remains a future target, the theoretical speedup is directly relevant to large-scale design space exploration in structural engineering.
  - Sources: [arXiv 2510.07280](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.07280)

- **Neural-Network-Assisted Genetic Algorithm for Production System Topology (2026)**: A hybrid framework combines discrete-event simulation with a neural network surrogate that predicts system performance metrics, feeding those predictions into a genetic algorithm for production system layout optimization. The neural surrogate reduces the number of expensive simulation calls by an order of magnitude while maintaining convergence to high-quality solutions, making it practical for real factory floor redesign problems.
  - Sources: [Taylor & Francis Online](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0951192X.2025.2455631)

- **GNNs as Surrogate Models for Physics-Based Geometry Design**: Graph neural networks trained on unstructured mesh or point cloud representations of 2D/3D geometries serve as direct surrogate models for physical simulations (stress, fluid flow, heat transfer), bypassing the need for explicit geometric parameterization. Bayesian optimization of the mesh resolution used in training further improves the accuracy-efficiency trade-off, enabling fast engineering performance prediction at design-exploration scale.
  - Sources: [GNN Surrogate for Physics Simulations (IEEE)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10022022/) · [Bayesian Mesh Optimization (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.01996)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **Bistable Origami-Inspired Soft Pneumatic Actuators for Legged Locomotion (BOSPA, SAGE Journals 2026)**: The BOSPA consists of a 3D-printed inflatable Miura-origami tube structure with two parallel air chambers and strategically placed elastic rings. The bistable geometry enables multiple distinct deformation modes — linear extension, bending, and coupled bending/extension — from a single actuator design by controlling chamber pressure differentials. This multiplicity of locomotion gaits from a minimally complex hardware design is demonstrated in a soft-legged robot, offering a new paradigm for programmable legged soft locomotion.
  - Sources: [SAGE Journals](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024)

- **Harvard Rotational Multi-Material 3D Printing — Programmable Artificial Muscle Filaments (PNAS 2026)**: (See also Additive Manufacturing section.) By continuously rotating a multi-material nozzle during printing, researchers at Harvard SEAS encode helical microstructure directly into filament geometry, creating temperature-responsive artificial muscles. The work demonstrates entangled injectable filaments that lock in place post-injection to form porous scaffolds, and reconfigurable grippers that gently manipulate delicate objects. This approach encodes actuation logic into structure rather than requiring embedded actuators or electronics, pointing toward fully printable soft robotic systems.
  - Sources: [PNAS (Tom's Hardware Coverage)](https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/harvard-researchers-develop-novel-3d-printing-method-for-soft-robotics-rotational-multi-material-method-creates-muscle-like-structures-that-can-be-programmed-to-twist-lift-or-bend) · [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/harvard-rotational-3d-printing-soft-robots/) · [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/science/3d-printing-technique-soft-filaments-robotics)

- **SOPA — Modular Soft Origami Pneumatic Actuator with Tunable Stiffness (ScienceDirect 2026)**: The SOPA integrates a foldable origami structure with a pneumatic pouch design, enabling bidirectional angular displacement up to 110° per module. Crucially, stiffness can be tuned independently of displacement by controlling the fold geometry and pressurization, making the actuator programmable for different load-bearing requirements. Modules are designed to stack and interconnect, providing a modular toolkit for assembling complex soft robotic systems from standardized pneumatic units.
  - Sources: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0020740325008033)

- **4D Printing of Magnetically Responsive Shape Memory Polymers (Advanced Science 2026)**: A comprehensive 2026 review in Advanced Science covers the fusion of 4D printing with magneto-responsive shape memory polymers (SMPs), enabling remote actuation through external magnetic fields combined with thermally triggered shape recovery. Primary fabrication routes include material extrusion, vat photopolymerization, and powder bed fusion. Applications span reconfigurable soft robotics, wearable thermal devices, morphing aerospace structures, and implantable biomedical devices, with the remote activation paradigm eliminating the need for tethered pneumatic or electrical connections.
  - Sources: [Advanced Science (Wiley)](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202513091)

- **High-Performance Elastic-Soft Hybrid Origami Actuator (ScienceDirect 2026)**: A hybrid pneumatic actuator combining elastic and soft components with an origami structure achieves maximum elongation exceeding 660% and maximum bending angles of 80°, with modules assembled into continuum robots capable of operating near their base. The combination of elastic energy storage with soft pneumatic compliance provides both large deformation range and controlled force output, addressing a fundamental trade-off in soft actuator design.
  - Sources: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0020740325000219)

- **4D-Printed Active Hinges for Deployable and Morphing Structures**: Researchers demonstrated active hinges fabricated via 4D printing of shape memory polymers and used them to construct a morphing wing flap and a deployable structural panel. The hinges are programmed to fold and unfold on thermal stimulation, enabling compact stowage and in-situ deployment without mechanical actuators. This approach provides a lightweight, single-material alternative to motor-driven joints for deployable aerospace and architectural structures.
  - Sources: [ACS Applied Polymer Materials](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsapm.5c02816) · [Shape Memory Polymer Composites (Science Partner Journals)](https://spj.science.org/doi/10.34133/research.0234)

- **PneumaGami — EPFL Origami-Inspired Modular Soft Robot**: The PneumaGami platform from EPFL exploits origami-inspired "waterbomb" base geometries and soft pneumatic actuators to achieve parallel kinematic mechanism modules with independently controlled legs. Selectively compliant flexure hinge elements between rigid panels define each module as a 6R spherical joint, providing a clean kinematic abstraction over the soft mechanics. The modular architecture enables rapid assembly of diverse robot morphologies from a standard hardware kit.
  - Sources: [EPFL Graph Search](https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/publication/59809d8f-8232-476e-9574-e6fcb75d13dd) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/340016196_Soft_pneumatic_actuator-driven_origami-inspired_modular_robotic_pneumagami)

## Biology & Medical Research

- **World's First Personalized CRISPR Base Editing Therapy (CHOP / Penn Medicine, 2025–2026)**: Infant KJ, born with severe carbamoyl phosphate synthetase 1 (CPS1) deficiency — a rare and typically fatal metabolic disorder — became the first human treated with a fully personalized CRISPR base-editing therapy. The therapy used base editing to correct a single-nucleotide mutation without cutting the DNA double strand, delivered via lipid nanoparticles targeting the liver. Designed and manufactured in approximately six months from genome sequencing to first dose, the case establishes that fully individualized gene therapies are clinically and logistically feasible. KJ is now growing well and thriving — a landmark for precision medicine.
  - Sources: [CHOP News Release](https://www.chop.edu/news/worlds-first-patient-treated-personalized-crispr-gene-editing-therapy-childrens-hospital) · [MIT Technology Review Breakthrough Technologies 2026](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/01/12/1129999/gene-editing-base-edited-baby-personalized-drugs-2026-breakthrough-technology/)

- **In Vivo CAR-T Cell Therapy — Eliminating Ex Vivo Manufacturing (2026)**: In vivo CAR-T platforms reprogram a patient's own T cells inside the body using engineered viral or nanoparticle delivery vehicles, bypassing the laborious, costly ex vivo cell expansion process that limits traditional CAR-T access. Kelonia Therapeutics' KLN-1010 showed MRD-negative (minimal residual disease–negative) responses in Phase 1 for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma. Multiple clinical trials are now recruiting, including a CD19/BAFF-R targeting trial for B cell leukemia/lymphoma opened in February 2026. The technology could reduce CAR-T treatment costs by an order of magnitude and make cellular immunotherapy accessible globally.
  - Sources: [Science (in vivo CAR-T)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ads8473) · [AJMC Clinical Results](https://www.ajmc.com/view/in-vivo-car-t-takes-center-stage-with-results-shared-for-3-mrd-negative-patients) · [PMC Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12427527/)

- **Miniaturized CRISPR — Al3Cas12f Enables AAV Delivery In Vivo (NIH, 2026)**: An NIH-funded team identified Al3Cas12f, a naturally occurring compact CRISPR effector enzyme small enough to be packaged into adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors — the gold-standard gene delivery vehicle for in vivo use. Prior CRISPR effectors (SpCas9, etc.) are too large for AAV packaging, requiring viral split-delivery or lipid nanoparticles.
