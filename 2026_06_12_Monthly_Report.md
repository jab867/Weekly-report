## Computational Design

- **Mamba-CAD: State Space Model for 3D CAD Generation (AAAI 2026)**: Mamba-CAD applies the Mamba state-space model (SSM) architecture—previously dominant in sequence modeling—to the problem of generating valid parametric CAD sequences. Unlike Transformer-based approaches that struggle with long CAD command sequences due to quadratic attention complexity, Mamba's linear-complexity recurrent processing handles longer and more complex part descriptions. The team built a new dataset of 77,078 CAD models with extended parametric sequences and demonstrated state-of-the-art generation quality under multiple evaluation metrics. This is particularly significant for design automation: longer, more complex parts that previously exceeded model capacity can now be generated end-to-end.
  - Sources: [AAAI 2026 Proceedings – Mamba-CAD](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/32531) · [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.00439) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390716842_Mamba-CAD_State_Space_Model_for_3D_Computer-Aided_Design_Generative_Modeling)

This positions Autodesk as pushing fabrication-aware design into mainstream workflows.
  - Sources: [Novatr – Best Generative Design Software 2026](https://www.novatr.com/blog/generative-design-softwares) · [CoLab – AI Tools for Mechanical Engineers](https://www.colabsoftware.com/guides/how-generative-design-works-a-guide-for-engineering-managers)

These models treat a CAD sketch as a structured language—a sequence of primitives and geometric constraints—enabling architectures that generate syntactically and semantically valid sketches for downstream 3D modeling.

## Computational Sciences

- **SOPTX: High-Performance Multi-Backend Topology Optimization Framework (Communications in Computational Physics, 2026)**: SOPTX is a new open-source topology optimization framework built on FEALPy that decouples the analysis and optimization stages and supports multiple computational backends—NumPy, PyTorch, and JAX—enabling both CPU and GPU execution with automatic differentiation for sensitivity computation. GPU computations reduce runtime to approximately 12% of CPU time, and a novel fast matrix assembly technique drops average assembly time from 0.838 s to 0.273 s. The modular architecture (Material, Analysis, Regularization, and Optimization modules) allows topology optimization for arbitrary dimensions and mesh types with minimal code changes. This is a significant infrastructure contribution that makes high-performance TO accessible without writing GPU kernels from scratch.
  - Sources: [Communications in Computational Physics – SOPTX](https://www.global-sci.com/cicp/article/view/24166) · [arXiv – SOPTX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02438) · [alphaXiv Overview](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.02438v1)

- **End-to-End Quantum Algorithm for Structural Topology Optimization (IOP Quantum Science & Technology, 2026)**: Researchers presented the first end-to-end, fault-tolerant quantum algorithm for topology optimization, demonstrated on the 2D Messerschmitt-Bölkow-Blohm beam problem. The algorithm reformulates compliance minimization as a combinatorial satisfiability problem and solves it with Grover's search algorithm, computing compliance via finite-element methods encoded in quantum circuits (block-encoding of the stiffness matrix, QSVT for matrix inversion, Hadamard test, and amplitude estimation). The key property: it evaluates exponentially many candidate structures in quantum superposition in polynomial time, achieving Grover's quadratic speedup. While fault-tolerant quantum hardware is still years away, this establishes a rigorous quantum-computational complexity baseline for structural optimization.
  - Sources: [IOPscience – Quantum TO Algorithm](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4cfe) · [arXiv 2510.07280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07280) · [RWTH Publications](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/1033016)

- **Deep Reinforcement Learning + Genetic Algorithms + FEM for Topology Optimization**: A new framework integrates DRL and genetic algorithms with on-the-fly FEM simulation—generating training data during RL episodes rather than requiring a pre-collected offline dataset. The approach was validated on rocket nozzle design, a high-value engineering case with complex thermal and structural constraints. By eliminating the dataset collection bottleneck and using FEM as the physics oracle, the system can optimize novel geometries without domain-specific training data.
  - Sources: [American Journal of Applied Scientific Research – DRL+GA+FEM TO](https://www.sciencepublishinggroup.com/article/10.11648/j.ajasr.20261201.11)

- **Training-Free Neural Topology Optimization via fKAN Reparameterization**: The fractional Kolmogorov-Arnold Network (fKAN) is adapted as a reparameterization layer for topology optimization, where learnable Jacobi polynomial activation parameters directly encode smooth density fields. Unlike prior neural-TO methods that require offline training on shape databases, fKAN-TO optimizes directly on the target problem via gradient descent. This yields a practical, dataset-free path to neural-accelerated topology optimization, particularly useful for novel materials or unconventional boundary conditions with no prior examples.
  - Sources: [ScienceDirect – fKAN TO Framework](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1474034625010043)

- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks with Combination Line Sampling**: A 2026 framework embeds governing PDEs and boundary conditions directly into neural network training while using combination line sampling to efficiently explore the structural response space with limited training data. The approach is shown to reliably approximate structural responses for reliability analysis—critical for probabilistic design where Monte Carlo simulation is prohibitively expensive. The integration of physical priors with sparse sampling addresses a key bottleneck in data-efficient surrogate modeling for engineering.
  - Sources: [Practical TO with DRL and GA](https://www.sciencepublishinggroup.com/article/10.11648/j.ajasr.20261201.11)

- **FEALPy: Cross-Platform Intelligent Numerical Simulation Engine**: FEALPy—the underlying numerical engine for SOPTX—has been published as a standalone cross-platform simulation framework supporting adaptive finite element methods across multiple backends. Its design as a backend-agnostic FEM library (CPU, GPU, distributed) positions it as infrastructure for next-generation simulation-driven design tools, including topology optimization, fluid simulation, and coupled multiphysics.
  - Sources: [arXiv – FEALPy](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06632)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **Meta-Ori: Monolithic Meta-Origami Inflatable Soft Actuators (RoboSoft 2025 / IEEE 2026)**: Meta-Ori combines nonlinear cylindrical mechanical metamaterials (metashells) with Kresling origami inflatable chambers to create tunable snap-through actuators printable in a single FDM step using commercial TPU filament—no manual assembly or post-print folding required. The large metamaterial design-parameter space allows fine-tuning of nonlinear force-displacement curves (stiffness, snap-through threshold, bistability) that traditional balloon or spring actuators cannot match. A companion Grasshopper parametric design tool allows users to specify geometry and preview nonlinear behavior before printing. Demonstrated applications include actuation sequencing from a single pressure input—relevant for deployable structures and multi-step grippers.
  - Sources: [IEEE Xplore – Meta-Ori](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11020947/) · [arXiv 2503.23375](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23375) · [SoftMachines Lab Paper](https://softmachineslab.org/assets/papers/oliveira_robosoft25.pdf)

- **Monolithic 3D Printing of Origami-Inspired Soft Robotics from Bio-Based Resin (PMC, 2026)**: Researchers demonstrated a single-step DLP printing process for complete origami-inflatable soft robotic grippers using a bio-based photopolymer resin formulated from acrylated epoxidized soybean oil and tetrahydrofurfuryl methacrylate. This eliminates multi-step assembly, folding, bonding, and post-cure processes that typically complicate soft robot fabrication. The bio-derived chemistry reduces the environmental footprint of soft robotic manufacturing—increasingly relevant as the field scales up production.
  - Sources: [PMC – Monolithic Origami Soft Robotics from Bio-Resin](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13042658/)

- **Highly Dynamic Origami Electrohydraulic Soft Actuators: Jumping 13.9 Body Heights (SmartBot, 2026)**: A new class of origami electrohydraulic (OES) soft actuators integrates rigid origami geometry with HASEL (hydraulically amplified self-healing electrostatic) actuation, enabling jumping to 13.9× body height and sustained locomotion at 0.17 body lengths/second. By coupling electrostatic dielectric fluid displacement with the geometric force amplification of origami folds, the actuators achieve blocking torques of 48.7 mN·m across a parametrically tunable 0°–163° bending range. The approach sidesteps the bandwidth penalty of pneumatic pump-and-pipe systems, enabling fast impulsive forces suited for locomotion and throwing tasks.
  - Sources: [Journal of Applied Physics – OES Actuators](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jap/article/139/2/025109/3377304/Origami-inspired-electrohydraulic-soft-actuators) · [SmartBot / Wiley – Dynamic EHO Actuators](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/smb2.70018)

- **Impulsive Actuation Review for Soft Robots (npj Robotics, 2025)**: A comprehensive review published in npj Robotics surveys strategies for achieving high-power-density, impulsive actuation in soft robots—enabling jumping over tall obstacles, launching projectiles, and pumping artificial hearts. The review covers HASEL actuators, pneumatic burst valves, stored elastic energy release, and thermally triggered snap-through. Key finding: combining fast-responsive actuation with passive energy storage and mechanical amplification (elastic mechanisms, geometric bistability) is the most promising path to soft robots that match or exceed biological muscle's peak power density.
  - Sources: [npj Robotics – Impulsive Actuation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44182-025-00045-0)

- **Auxetic Meta-Structure Enabled Soft Locomotive Robot (npj Robotics, 2025)**: Researchers designed a soft robot body using auxetic (negative-Poisson-ratio) metamaterial structures that expand laterally when stretched longitudinally, enabling a locomotion gait that exploits the coupled deformation for directional movement. The auxetic geometry is 3D printable and generates anisotropic friction automatically without added appendages or surface treatments. This approach shows how mechanical metamaterial design directly enables functional robot behavior.
  - Sources: [npj Robotics – Auxetic Meta-Structure Robot](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44182-025-00050-3)

- **Programmable Somatosensory Soft Robots (npj Flexible Electronics, 2026)**: A 2026 paper introduces soft robots with distributed, programmable somatosensory networks—sensor arrays that mimic the spatial and functional organization of biological skin, giving robots real-time proprioception and contact mapping across their bodies.
