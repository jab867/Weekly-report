## Computational Design

- **MIT GenCAD — image-conditioned parametric CAD generation**: MIT researchers unveiled GenCAD on May 18, a generative AI model that takes a single image or sketch as input and outputs the complete parametric CAD command history — not a mesh or point cloud, but the actual sequence of solid-modeling operations that recreates the part as a fully editable CAD program. The architecture uses a three-stage pipeline: a transformer-based contrastive representation module aligns the latent spaces of CAD command sequences and images; a latent diffusion model generates CAD latents conditioned on the image embedding; and a decoder translates latents back to executable CAD commands compatible with standard geometry kernels. GenCAD significantly outperforms prior methods on both conditional and unconditional CAD generation and also enables image-based CAD retrieval from large databases — a powerful capability for reverse-engineering physical objects.
  - Sources: [MIT GenCAD AIToolly](https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-05-18-mit-researchers-introduce-gencad-a-generative-ai-model-for-image-conditioned-parametric-cad-program) · [GenCAD 3Druck](https://3druck.com/en/programs/ki-model-gencad-from-with-creates-editable-cad-programs-from-images-and-sketches-04157391/) · [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.16294) · [Sesame Disk](https://sesamedisk.com/gencad-image-conditioned-cad-generation/)

## 3D Printing / Additive Manufacturing

- **Additive manufacturing of cemented carbide — hardness above 1400 HV**: Researchers demonstrated a route to 3D print cemented carbide (WC-Co, one of the hardest engineering materials) using a hot-wire laser irradiation strategy while preserving hardness levels above 1400 HV — comparable to conventionally manufactured tooling. The key enabler is a nickel alloy-based intermediate layer combined with precise thermal control: temperatures are held above cobalt's melting point but below the grain growth threshold for tungsten carbide, preventing the hardness-degrading microstructural changes that plagued earlier AM attempts. This breakthrough could enable on-demand printing of cutting tools, wear-resistant inserts, and forming dies — components traditionally requiring long lead times from specialized manufacturers.
  - Sources: [Scientists Found a Way to 3D Print One of the Hardest Metals](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/03/260313002642.htm) · [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1115612)

- **Harvard rotational multi-material printing for muscle-like soft actuators**: Harvard researchers developed a rotational multi-material extrusion method that creates muscle-like structures programmable to twist, lift, or bend, directly targeting soft robotics applications. Unlike standard layer-by-layer deposition, the rotational approach deposits multiple materials in helical and circumferential patterns that mimic the fiber architecture of biological muscle, producing anisotropic mechanical responses tunable by changing deposition angle. The method bridges additive manufacturing and soft robotics by making the printing process itself encode actuator behavior — the geometry of deposition determines the motion.
  - Sources: [Harvard Rotational Multi-Material Method](https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/harvard-researchers-develop-novel-3d-printing-method-for-soft-robotics-rotational-multi-material-method-creates-muscle-like-structures-that-can-be-programmed-to-twist-lift-or-bend)

- **Multimaterial soft and stretchable electronics via two-photon polymerization**: A new multimaterial two-photon polymerization (2PP) resin integrates conducting polymer PEDOT:PSS and multi-walled carbon nanotubes within a hydrogel PEGDA matrix, achieving electrical conductivity of 1.4 × 10⁵ S m⁻¹ alongside over 80% optical transmittance — a combination previously unavailable in a single printable material. This resolves a longstanding tension in soft electronics: high conductivity typically requires opaque filler concentrations that sacrifice transparency, while transparent hydrogels have poor electrical performance. The 2PP platform's micrometer resolution makes this material directly applicable to biointerfaces, implantable sensors, and microscale energy harvesting devices.
  - Sources: [Advanced Science Paper](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202513208?af=R) · [Multi-Material 3D Printing 2026](https://www.sovol3d.com/blogs/news/multi-material-3d-printing-in-2026)

- **Flexible multi-material printing beyond layer-by-layer — SLA + Xolography hybrid**: A flexible hybrid fabrication strategy sequentially combines masked stereolithography (MSLA) with Xolography (a volumetric printing technique using intersecting light beams) to overcome the layer-by-layer constraint of conventional multi-material printing. This enables smooth material transitions and complex internal geometries that are inaccessible to nozzle-based or vat-based methods operating independently. The expanded material scope is particularly relevant for soft robotic components requiring graded stiffness transitions between rigid structural elements and compliant actuating regions.
  - Sources: [Advanced Materials Technologies](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/admt.202502447?af=R) · [Multi-Material 3D Printing 2026](https://www.sovol3d.com/blogs/news/multi-material-3d-printing-in-2026)

- **KIT CeraMMAM — multi-material ceramics in a single AM process**: The Karlsruhe Institute of Technology's CeraMMAM project developed a system producing high-performance ceramic and cermet components from multiple materials in a single additive manufacturing process using a universal binder system. Applications span medicine (implants, dental prosthetics), mechanical engineering (cutting tools, wear parts), and aerospace (thermal barrier components). The universal binder approach is architecturally significant because it decouples the printing process from material-specific chemistry, allowing material combinations that were previously impossible in a single print job.

The study characterizes how internal microstructure, residual stresses, and surface roughness from LPBF interact to drive crack initiation and propagation, providing predictive models for fatigue life that are currently missing from most AM certification frameworks. This work directly supports the industry-wide push toward certified flight hardware and structural medical implants from LPBF processes.

The technology extrudes metal-filled polymer rods, then debinds and sinters the resulting green part — a process analogous to desktop FDM but producing fully dense metal components. This democratization is accelerating prototyping cycles for structural components and enabling in-house tooling fabrication at organizations that previously relied entirely on contract manufacturing.

## Computational Sciences

- **SOPTX — modular topology optimization framework with GPU multi-backend support**: SOPTX, published in Communications in Computational Physics (Vol. 40(2), pp. 525–572, 2026), is a modular topology optimization package built on the FEALPy finite element library that decouples the analysis layer from the optimization layer through a non-intrusive design paradigm. It supports multiple computational backends — NumPy, PyTorch, and JAX — enabling seamless switching between CPU and GPU execution and leveraging automatic differentiation for gradient computation. The multi-backend approach allows researchers to prototype algorithms in NumPy and deploy them on GPU clusters without rewriting, significantly lowering the barrier to GPU-accelerated topology optimization research.
  - Sources: [SOPTX Communications in Computational Physics](https://www.global-sci.com/cicp/article/view/24166) · [SOPTX alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.02438v1)

- **Deep reinforcement learning + genetic algorithms + FEM for topology optimization**: A new framework integrates deep reinforcement learning (DRL), genetic algorithms (GA), and finite element methods (FEM) in a unified pipeline for topology optimization of engineering structures. The critical distinction from prior AI-driven topology optimization is that RL and GA agents generate training data by running physics simulations during training, rather than requiring a pre-existing dataset — enabling the system to explore design spaces that have never been explicitly sampled. This self-generating data loop makes the approach applicable to novel material systems or boundary conditions where no prior optimization database exists.
  - Sources: [DRL and GA Topology Optimization](https://www.sciencepublishinggroup.com/article/10.11648/j.ajasr.20261201.11) · [Physics-Informed TO](https://www.astesj.com/v06/i04/p27/)

- **Quantum algorithm for topology optimization via Grover's search**: An end-to-end, fault-tolerant quantum algorithm for structural topology optimization reformulates compliance minimization as a combinatorial satisfiability problem and solves it using Grover's algorithm on an exponentially large Hilbert space representing the design space. The algorithm incorporates quantum singular value transformation for structural analysis and quantum amplitude estimation for objective evaluation, achieving a theoretical quadratic speedup over classical exhaustive search. While fault-tolerant quantum hardware at the required qubit count remains years away, this result establishes the theoretical scaling advantage and is the first complete end-to-end quantum topology optimization pipeline with verified correctness.
  - Sources: [End-to-End Quantum Topology Optimization](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4cfe) · [Variational Quantum Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.07099)

- **Quantum neural network-assisted topology optimization with parameterized circuits**: A separate study in Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering demonstrates quantum neural network (QNN)-assisted topology optimization using parameterized quantum circuits (PQCs), where the QNN acts as a surrogate for the finite element compliance computation during gradient-based optimization. The approach is currently demonstrable on near-term NISQ devices for small problem instances, unlike the fault-tolerant Grover approach. The authors show that PQC-based surrogates can generalize across boundary condition variations, suggesting potential for multi-scenario optimization in a single quantum training pass.
  - Sources: [QNN Topology Optimization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/article/pii/S0045782525006838) · [SOPTX Paper](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.02438v1)

- **Physics-informed neural networks with hard constraints for inverse design**: A SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing paper presents a PINN framework with hard boundary and physics constraints for inverse design problems, addressing the well-known failure mode where standard PINNs violate physical constraints at training boundary points. By embedding constraints directly into the network architecture rather than as soft penalty terms, the method guarantees constraint satisfaction during inference rather than only in expectation. This is particularly relevant for structural inverse design and additive manufacturing process optimization, where constraint violations (e.g., exceeding yield stress, violating minimum feature size) are not acceptable even as rare events.
  - Sources: [PINNs Hard Constraints SIAM](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/21M1397908)

- **Shape-adaptive programmable morphing structures via SMA–SMP integration**: A 2026 study in Soft Robotics (SAGE) proposes an integrated control strategy for actuators combining shape memory alloys (SMA) and shape memory polymers (SMP), using a multi-target thermal sensing method (MTTSM) to coordinate the two distinct thermal response profiles of the two materials. SMA provides fast, high-force actuation while SMP provides programmable stiffness locking — together enabling a structure that can both actuate rapidly and freeze in arbitrary intermediate configurations. The framework enables preheating standby, stepped deformation sequences with programmed geometries, and dynamic stiffness modulation from a single thermal input signal.
  - Sources: [Shape-Adaptive Robotics SMA-SMP](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261437932) · [Soft Robotics Actuator Landscape 2026](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/articles/soft-robotics-actuator-technology-landscape-2026/)

## Soft Robotics & Compliant Mechanisms

- **BOSPA — bistable origami soft pneumatic actuator with snap-through locomotion**: Published in SAGE Journals in February 2026, this paper introduces a bistable origami soft pneumatic actuator (BOSPA) based on a 3D-printed inflatable Miura-origami tube structure with two parallel air chambers and strategically placed elastic rings in horizontal and vertical orientations. By programming air pressure differentials between the two chambers, the actuator achieves multiple deformation modes — linear extension, planar bending, and coupled bending/extension — each transitioning via snap-through bistability that amplifies output force and enables rapid state changes. The authors designed four distinct soft robots using different numbers of BOSPA modules, demonstrating versatile locomotion gaits ranging from undulatory crawling to inchworm-like stepping, all driven by the same fundamental actuator architecture.
  - Sources: [BOSPA SAGE Journals](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/21695172261424024)

- **SOPA — modular soft origami pneumatic actuator with programmable stiffness**: The Soft Origami Pneumatic Actuator (SOPA) integrates a foldable origami structure with a pneumatic pouch design to achieve bidirectional angular displacement of up to 110° per module from a single pneumatic input. Stiffness and motion range are independently programmable by adjusting the origami fold pattern geometry before assembly, and modular stacking of SOPA units enables total angular displacement up to 360° — sufficient for a full joint revolution in a soft robotic arm. The combination of programmable stiffness and high angular range in a single-input soft actuator significantly simplifies control systems compared to multi-actuator designs requiring independent pressure regulation.
  - Sources: [SOPA ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0020740325008033) · [Soft Pneumatic Actuators Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12197161/)

- **SMA–SMP programmable morphing structures for shape-adaptive robotics**: A 2026 paper in SAGE Journals demonstrates a programmable morphing structure combining shape memory alloys (SMA) and shape memory polymers (SMP) controlled by a Multi-Target Thermal Sensing Method (MTTSM) that monitors and coordinates the distinct thermal activation profiles of the two materials. SMA provides reversible, high-force actuation at the actuator's primary working temperature, while SMP locks the deformed geometry at a programmed intermediate configuration, effectively functioning as a structural memory that holds shape without continuous power input. The coordinated control framework enables standby preheating, stepped deformation sequences, and dynamic stiffness modulation — addressing the longstanding challenge of energy-efficient shape-locking in soft robotic systems.

The approach uses a custom multimaterial extrusion platform that deposits soft silicone (structure), conductive carbon-loaded silicone (sensing), and thermoplastic elastomer (strain-limiting) in a single coordinated print job. Embedding sensors during printing rather than attaching them afterward dramatically improves sensing fidelity, as embedded sensors deform confomally with the structure rather than creating stress concentrations at attachment points.

The review identifies integration of sensing (resistive, capacitive, optical) into fabric actuator walls as the primary frontier for making FBPSAs into smart, feedback-capable systems rather than purely open-loop actuators.

The origami skeleton provides geometric amplification of the pneumatic pressure force while the elastic skin provides restoring force and sealing, enabling a compact actuator that outperforms bulkier designs. The hybrid architecture design principle — using rigid-foldable structures to amplify soft actuator output — is emerging as a general strategy for bridging the force gap between soft and rigid robotic systems.

## Key Takeaways

- **Personalized medicine crossed a threshold**: The one-year follow-up on KJ Muldoon's CRISPR CPS1 therapy — coupled with reports of a separate personalized mRNA CRISPR therapy delivered within six months of diagnosis — marks a genuine inflection point where bespoke genetic medicine is transitioning from a proof-of-concept curiosity to an emerging clinical practice. The model of designing, manufacturing, and delivering a patient-specific gene edit within six months could eventually be applied to any Mendelian disease with a known causal variant, regardless of patient population size. [CHOP One-Year Report](https://www.chop.edu/news/childrens-hospital-philadelphia-marks-one-year-anniversary-worlds-first-personalized-crispr)

- **AI-to-CAD pipelines are maturing from static mesh generation to editable parametric programs**: MIT GenCAD and Autodesk Neural CAD both output parametric CAD command histories rather than triangulated meshes, and SolidWorks 2026's AI Virtual Companions embed AI reasoning directly into the constraint-editing workflow. This matters because downstream fabrication — CNC machining, FDM, injection molding — requires semantically correct constraint networks, not just surface geometry; a model that generates a mesh cannot be directly manufactured from that output without substantial manual reconstruction. The convergence of generative AI with parametric CAD programs is the decisive architectural step that makes AI a first-class participant in design-to-fabrication pipelines. [MIT GenCAD](https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-05-18-mit-researchers-introduce-gencad-a-generative-ai-model-for-image-conditioned-parametric-cad-program)

- **NVIDIA GR00T N2's World Action Model represents a new robotics paradigm**: The shift from reactive sensorimotor policies to predictive internal simulation — robots that mentally rehearse a grasp before executing it — is architecturally significant in the same way that chain-of-thought reasoning was for LLMs. The Transformer-Mamba hybrid that handles both high-level semantic planning and 1 kHz low-level motor control in a single model is a direct analog to how frontier LLMs now handle both strategic reasoning and syntactic generation. Combined with GR00T N1.7's open release and Cosmos 3's synthetic data generation, this represents a complete ecosystem for training general-purpose manipulation policies. [GR00T N2 Overview](https://robocloud-dashboard.vercel.app/learn/blog/gr00t-world-action-models-2026)

- **Multi-material 3D printing is enabling structures that are simultaneously soft, conductive, and load-bearing**: The PEDOT:PSS/MWCNT/PEGDA resin (1.4 × 10⁵ S m⁻¹ conductivity, 80% optical transmittance), Harvard's rotational muscle-like printing, and KIT's universal ceramic binder system collectively indicate that the fundamental material combination constraints of additive manufacturing are being systematically removed. Soft robots with embedded sensing, transparent implantable electronics, and multi-material ceramic/metal structural components are no longer experimental — they are becoming printable in a single process. This closes the gap between what can be designed computationally and what can be fabricated physically. [Advanced Science Paper](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202513208?af=R)

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash demonstrates that efficiency, capability, and low cost are no longer mutually exclusive**: At 289 tokens/second, 10× cheaper than Opus 4.7, and leading on multimodal and tool-use benchmarks, Gemini 3.5 Flash makes it practical to deploy frontier-class AI in latency-sensitive and cost-sensitive applications that were previously accessible only to well-funded teams. For research workflows specifically — automated literature review, design space exploration, simulation result interpretation — the combination of multimodal understanding and low cost per query changes what is economically feasible to automate. [Full Benchmark Comparison](https://apidog.com/blog/gemini-3-5-vs-gpt-5-5-vs-opus-4-7/)

- **Topology optimization is converging with quantum computing and deep learning simultaneously**: The same month saw SOPTX (GPU-accelerated TO framework with auto-differentiation backends), a DRL+GA+FEM self-generating data pipeline, and two separate quantum algorithms for topology optimization (one fault-tolerant Grover's approach, one near-term NISQ QNN approach). [SOPTX](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.02438v1) · [Quantum Topology Optimization](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ae4cfe)

- **Origami-inspired soft actuators are converging on industrial viability through bistability and modularity**: The BOSPA's snap-through bistability (amplified output force from pneumatic input) and SOPA's 360° angular range from modular stacking both address the two main practical limitations of origami soft actuators in deployed systems: low force output and limited range of motion. Bistable origami transitions provide a mechanical advantage that multiplies actuator output without additional pneumatic pressure, while modular stacking enables arbitrary kinematic chains from a single standardized actuator building block. These design principles are generalizable across scales and materials, pointing toward origami soft actuators as a manufacturable, force-competitive alternative to rigid servo-driven mechanisms.
